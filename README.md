<h1>🏛️ Barangay CINCO Official Portal</h1>
<p>A responsive, modern web portal designed for Barangay CINCO (Bacoor City, Cavite). This platform serves as a centralized hub for residents to access historical background, upcoming community announcements, real-time weather tracking, service application workflows, and directory profiles for local officials.</p>
<br>
<h2>✨ Features</h2>
<h3>📰 Interactive History & Blog:</h3>
<p>A beautifully styled history section with drop-caps, responsive content grids, and structural mission/vision blocks highlighting the community's core values.</p>
<h3>📢 Real-Time Community Calendar:</h3>
<p>A cleanly organized monthly events schedule paired with an active upcoming alerts sidebar tracker.</p>
<h3>🛡️ Safety & Environmental Monitor:</h3>
<p>Essential disaster readiness protocols backed up by a full-frame, live satellite and weather layout tracking regional typhoons.</p>
<h3>📄 Document Application Guide & Contact:</h3>
<p>A clear multi-step guide explaining processing requirements alongside an interactive contact submission form.</p>
<h3>⚖️ Council Directory:</h3>
<p>A beautifully structured administrative layout highlighting profile records and active roles of barangay officials.</p>
<br>
<h2>🎯 Our Mission</h2>
<p>Our mission is to serve every resident with <b>Compassion</b> and <b>Integrity</b>, ensuring public safety and <b>Order</b> through effective local governance. We are driven by a deep sense of <b>Nationalism</b> and an unwavering <b>Commitment</b> to uplifting lives by providing excellent public services, fostering sustainable development, and empowering our youth to become the next generation of leaders.</p>
<h2>👁️ Our Vision</h2>
<p>To be a <b>safe, progressive, and unified barangay</b> where disciplined citizens thrive in a clean environment, guided by honest leadership and a shared love for our community and nation.</p>
<h2>💎 Core Values</h2>
<p>The foundational principles of our community form the acronym C.I.N.C.O.</p>
<li><b>Compassion:</b> Serving every resident, especially those in need, with kindness, empathy, and active support.</li>
<li><b>Integrity::</b> Maintaining transparency, honesty, and accountability in all government transactions and leadership.</li>
<li><b>Nationalism:</b> Fostering unity and pride in our local heritage, culture, and love for the country.</li>
<li><b>Commitment:</b> Working dedicatedly to deliver consistent, dependable, and high-quality public services to the community.</li>
<li><b>Order:</b> Ensuring peace, safety, and proper discipline through fair implementation of local rules and ordinances.</li>
